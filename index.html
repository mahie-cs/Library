About

Library is a free, central hub for engineering and science books. I made it for the students of SIMT Engineering College and every student studying there. Any student around the world is welcome to use it and benefit from it.

FreeI don't earn any money from this project.
No adsNothing here is trying to sell you anything.
No data collectionNothing about you is tracked or stored.

I built this so that students who need a book can find it in one place. It will keep growing as more books are added.
