> **Incomplete!** This project is still a work in progress.

An online library for browsing engineering and science books in PDF format.

Created by **MD. Montasir Rahman Mahie**.

## Features

- **Book covers:** each book is shown with its cover, taken from the first page of the PDF.
- **Fast loading:** covers are small pre-made images, and only the books you can see on screen are loaded.
- **Book details:** click a book to see its cover, title, authors and edition number, with a link to read the PDF.
- **Search:** find books by title or author.
- **Categories:** books are grouped by topic. Click a topic to see the books in it.
- **Light and dark mode:** the site follows your device's theme.
- **Works on phones and computers.**
